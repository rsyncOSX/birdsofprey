The Eurasian pygmy owl is Europe's smallest owl and can be found in the forests of Norway. Despite its small size, it is a capable hunter, feeding on small mammals and birds.

## Feeding

The Eurasian pygmy owl hunts small mammals, including voles and mice, as well as small birds. It watches for prey from a perch before swooping down to catch it.
